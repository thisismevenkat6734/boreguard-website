About BoreGuard

BoreGuard is an innovative citizen-driven safety platform that empowers citizens to report dangerous open borewells using GPS, maps and cloud technology. Every report helps authorities identify hazards faster and prevent tragic accidents. Our vision is to build the world's largest borewell safety reporting platform and save lives through technology and community participation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to eliminate accidents caused by open borewells by enabling instant reporting, accurate location tracking and faster response from authorities. Together we can build a safer future.
