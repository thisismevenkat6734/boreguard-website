About BoreGuard

BoreGuard is an innovative citizen-driven safety platform designed to identify, report and monitor dangerous open borewells using GPS, cloud technology and interactive maps. Every report submitted by a citizen helps authorities respond faster and reduces the risk of accidents. Our vision is to build the world's largest borewell safety reporting platform and protect lives through technology and community participation.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make every community safer by enabling citizens to report dangerous open borewells instantly, helping authorities respond quickly, and reducing the risk of accidents through technology and community participation.

How BoreGuard Works

1️⃣ Report Borewell

Citizens identify a dangerous open borewell and submit a report through BoreGuard.

2️⃣ GPS Location

The application automatically captures the precise GPS location of the borewell.

3️⃣ Upload Photo

A real-time photo is uploaded to help authorities verify the report quickly.

4️⃣ Authority Response

The report becomes available for review, enabling authorities to take appropriate action.

5️⃣ Improve Public Safety

Timely reporting and response help reduce risks and create safer communities.
