About BoreGuard

BoreGuard is an innovative citizen-driven safety platform created to identify, report and monitor dangerous open borewells. By combining GPS, maps, cloud technology and community participation, BoreGuard aims to reduce accidents and help authorities respond quickly. Every report submitted by a citizen can help prevent a tragedy and protect lives.

Our Mission

Every open borewell is a potential danger. Our mission is to build a safer India by empowering citizens to report dangerous borewells instantly and help authorities take timely action. Together, we can prevent accidents and save precious lives.
